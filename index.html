ClipQueue

ClipQueue is a personal desktop tool that helps one creator publish short vertical videos to their own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts.

How it works

  1. The creator prepares short clips on their own computer.
  2. Each clip is sent to the creator for review. Nothing is published without the creator's explicit approval.
  3. After approval, ClipQueue uploads the clip to the creator's connected accounts using the official YouTube Data API, TikTok Content Posting API and Instagram Graph API.

ClipQueue is not a public service; it is used only by its operator on their own accounts.
