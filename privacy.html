Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

ClipQueue ("the app") is a personal tool run by Filip Tichý. It is used only to publish videos to the operator's own social media accounts.

Information the app accesses

  • Access tokens issued by Google/YouTube, TikTok and Meta (Instagram) after you sign in and grant permission.
  • Basic profile information needed to post: TikTok display name and avatar, Instagram account ID and username, YouTube channel used for upload.
  • The videos and captions you approve for publishing.

How the information is used

Only to upload the videos you approve to the accounts you connected, and to show you the resulting post links. The app does not read your messages, followers, viewing history or any other account data.

Storage and sharing

Tokens and videos are stored only on the operator's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics. Data is sent only to the platform you publish to (YouTube, TikTok or Instagram).

YouTube API Services

ClipQueue uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. ClipQueue's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke access at any time: Google at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions, TikTok in Settings → Security → Manage app permissions, Instagram/Facebook in Settings → Apps and Websites. Revoking access makes stored tokens unusable; you may also request deletion of any stored data by email and it will be deleted within 7 days.
